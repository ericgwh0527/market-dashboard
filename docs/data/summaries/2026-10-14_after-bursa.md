**Concept of the day**
The **RSI (Relative Strength Index)** is a technical indicator that measures momentum on a scale of 0 to 100 to show if a stock is overbought or oversold. Generally, an RSI reading above 70 suggests a stock has run up very fast and might be due for a pullback, while a reading below 30 indicates it may be oversold.
