**Concept of the day: RSI (Relative Strength Index)**  
RSI is a momentum indicator measured on a scale from 0 to 100 that tracks how fast and aggressively a stock's price has moved recently. A reading above 70 suggests a stock may be overbought after running up quickly, while a level below 30 indicates it is oversold after heavy selling.
