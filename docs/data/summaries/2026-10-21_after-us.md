**Concept of the day: RSI (Relative Strength Index)**  
RSI is a technical indicator measured on a scale from 0 to 100 that gauges how quickly and aggressively traders are buying or selling an asset. A reading below 30 typically suggests an asset is "oversold" and may have dropped too fast, while a reading above 70 indicates it may be "overbought."
