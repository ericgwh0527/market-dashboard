**Concept of the day: Relative Strength Index (RSI)**  
The Relative Strength Index (RSI) is a momentum indicator that measures the speed and size of recent price changes on a scale from 0 to 100. A reading below 30 typically suggests an asset is "oversold" (heavily sold down), while a reading above 70 indicates it may be "overbought."
