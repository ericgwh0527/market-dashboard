**Concept of the day**
**RSI (Relative Strength Index):** A momentum indicator that measures the speed and change of price movements on a scale of 0 to 100. An RSI above 70 typically suggests a stock is "overbought" and might be due for a pullback, while an RSI below 30 indicates it is "oversold."
