**Concept of the day**  
**RSI (Relative Strength Index)** is a technical indicator that measures the speed and change of price movements on a scale from 0 to 100. An RSI reading above 70 typically suggests an asset is "overbought" and might be due for a pause or pullback, while a reading below 30 suggests it is "oversold."
