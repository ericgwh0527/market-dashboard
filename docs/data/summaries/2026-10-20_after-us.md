**Concept of the day**  
The **Relative Strength Index (RSI)** is a momentum indicator measured on a scale from 0 to 100 that tracks how fast and hard a stock's price has moved recently. A reading below 30 typically signals that an asset is "oversold," meaning selling pressure has been unusually intense.
