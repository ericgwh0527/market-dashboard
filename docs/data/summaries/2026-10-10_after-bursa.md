**Concept of the day: RSI (Relative Strength Index)**  
RSI is a momentum indicator measured on a scale from 0 to 100 that tracks the speed and change of recent price movements. A reading above 70 typically suggests an asset is "overbought" (rallied quickly), while a reading below 30 signals it may be "oversold" (sold off heavily).
