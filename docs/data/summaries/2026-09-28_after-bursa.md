**Concept of the day**  
The **Relative Strength Index (RSI)** is a technical indicator measured from 0 to 100 that gauges the speed and magnitude of recent price changes. An RSI reading above 70 (seen in AMD at 73.0 and Meta at 71.3) typically indicates an asset is "overbought" and may have run up too quickly.
