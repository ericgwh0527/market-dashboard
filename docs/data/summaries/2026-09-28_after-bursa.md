**Concept of the day**  
The **Relative Strength Index (RSI)** is a momentum indicator measured on a scale of 0 to 100 that shows whether an asset has moved too fast in either direction. A reading above 70, such as AMD's 73 or Meta's 71, suggests a stock is technically overbought in the short term.
