**Concept of the day**  
**RSI (Relative Strength Index)** is a momentum indicator that measures the speed and change of price movements on a scale from 0 to 100. Traditionally, a reading above 70 suggests an asset may be overbought, while a reading below 30 indicates it may be oversold.
