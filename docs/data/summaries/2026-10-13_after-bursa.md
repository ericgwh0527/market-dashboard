**Concept of the day**  
A **golden cross** is a chart pattern where a short-term moving average (like the 50-day) crosses above a long-term moving average (like the 200-day). Investors often view it as a signal that recent buying momentum is outpacing long-term trends, suggesting potential continued strength.
