**Concept of the day**  
The **Relative Strength Index (RSI)** is a momentum indicator scored from 0 to 100 that measures the speed and size of recent price changes. A reading below 30 typically suggests an asset is "oversold" (heavily dumped), which some traders monitor for potential price stabilization.
