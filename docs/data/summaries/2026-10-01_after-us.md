**Concept of the day**  
**Relative Strength Index (RSI):** A momentum indicator measured on a scale from 0 to 100 that evaluates how quickly and sharply investors have been buying or selling an asset. Generally, a reading above 70 indicates a stock may be overbought (potentially due for a cooldown), while a reading below 30 suggests it is oversold.
