**Concept of the day**
The **RSI (Relative Strength Index)** is a momentum indicator used to measure whether a stock is overbought or oversold on a scale of 0 to 100. Generally, an RSI reading below 30 suggests a stock has been heavily sold off and might be oversold, while a reading above 70 indicates it may be overbought.
