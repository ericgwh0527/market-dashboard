**Concept of the day**  
The **Relative Strength Index (RSI)** is a momentum indicator scaled from 0 to 100 that measures the speed and magnitude of recent price changes. A reading below 30 (like Tenaga’s 22.56) generally suggests an asset has been sold off heavily and may be oversold.
