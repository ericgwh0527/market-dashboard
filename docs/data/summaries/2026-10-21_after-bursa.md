**Concept of the day: Death Cross**  
A death cross occurs when a stock's short-term trend line (commonly the 50-day moving average) crosses below its long-term trend line (the 200-day moving average). Investors track this chart event because it signals that recent momentum has weakened relative to the past year, indicating a potential ongoing downtrend.
